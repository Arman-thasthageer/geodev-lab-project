GeoDev Africa — Week 1 Project Brief
1. Question
Which settlements in Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu, are more than 5 km by road from the nearest public health facility?
2. Why it matters
Access to healthcare can vary considerably between settlements depending on their location and the available road network. Identifying settlements that are more than 5 km by road from the nearest public health facility can help highlight areas with potentially limited healthcare accessibility.
This analysis will provide a simple spatial assessment of healthcare access in Villupuram district and identify settlements that may require further investigation or improved connectivity.
3. Data needed
The project requires the following datasets:
Villupuram district boundary
Used to define the study area.
Settlement / village locations
Used to identify the settlements included in the analysis.
Public health facility locations
Used as the destination locations for healthcare accessibility analysis.
Road network
Used to calculate distance from settlements to the nearest health facility by road.
Population data
Used to understand the population associated with settlements with limited healthcare access.

5. What I will build
I will build a GIS-based healthcare accessibility analysis for Villupuram district. The workflow will combine settlement locations, public health facilities, and the road network to identify settlements that are more than 5 km by road from their nearest public health facility.
The final output will be a map showing settlement accessibility categories, supported by basic statistics describing the number and population of settlements with limited road access to healthcare facilities.
